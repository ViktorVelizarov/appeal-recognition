§ I — Method

Three movements, one photograph.

  1. I

    Detect

    A vision model finds every garment and accessory in the photo and scores its confidence.

  2. II

    Match

    Each piece is searched across online shops for the same item, then for close alternatives.

  3. III

    Buy

    Shop, price and a direct link for every match, closest first.
